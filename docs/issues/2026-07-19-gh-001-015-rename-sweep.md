## Root-cause hypotheses

1. The `pd-*` to `pdomain-*` rename superseded the earlier `pd-index` to
   `pd-index-pip` rename while these tracking issues were still open, and the
   defining plan was deleted or moved during that transition. Confirming this
   needs a search of Git history in whichever repository once held
   `docs/superpowers/plans/`.

## Defects to fix

1. Fifteen tracking issues point at a plan file that no longer exists, so their
   intended work is not recoverable from the issues alone.
1. Legacy `pd-*` references persist across the workspace with no live owner or
   tracker.

## Next steps

The owner picks one:

1. **Re-scope and re-home.** Treat the cluster as dead and file fresh sweep work
   against current `pdomain-*` naming, most plausibly on `pdomain-ops` rather
   than here, since the remaining files live in other repositories.
1. **Close as superseded.** Accept that the `pdomain-*` rename achieved the
   intent for this repository, and that lingering references elsewhere are
   acceptable or tracked separately.
1. **Reconstruct the plan.** Recover or rewrite
   `2026-05-16-pd-index-rename-to-pip.md` from Git history in whichever
   repository once held it, then re-derive per-repo tasks.

Until one is chosen, this record stays `needs-owner-decision`. Do not treat it
as active work.
